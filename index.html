How to Craft the Best Jewels

A compact 5-mod method for Emeralds and Sapphires.

Emeralds Sapphires

    What you will need

  • Fractured Crit Strike Damage base (20%)
  • Chaos Orbs
  • Potent Liquid Contempt
  • Preserved Cranium
  • Abyssal Echoes
  • Omens of Light
  • Sinistral Annulment
  • Exalted Orbs
  • Potent Liquid of Ferocity
01BASE

Fracture Critical Strike Damage

Buy or fracture Critical Strike Damage. You want 20%.

02SUFFIX

Chaos for Critical Hit Chance

Chaos spam for Increased Critical Hit Chance.

03LIQUID

Potent Liquid Contempt

Confirm the jewel has 4 modifiers, then use Potent Liquid Contempt.

Risk: 33% chance to remove your crit. Restart if that happens.

04UNVEIL

Desecrate until Attack Speed

Use Preserved Cranium with Abyssal Echoes and Omens of Light until you unveil Attack Speed.

Spell builds can unveil a second Crit Damage mod here instead.

05ANNUL

Sinistral Annulment

Once the desired mod is on, annul the prefix with a Sinistral Annulment.

Miss: if you miss the +1 suffix, you will have to do this twice.

06EXALT

Slam to Five Affixes

Once the +1 suffix crafted mod is removed, exalt slam until the jewel has 5 affixes.

07PREFIX

Chaos for One Good Prefix

Chaos spam for one good prefix:

Projectile Damage · Elemental Damage · Damage vs Rares · Attack Damage

08FINISH

Potent Liquid of Ferocity

Use Potent Liquid of Ferocity. This is a 50/50 to finish the craft.

If you hit your good mod, chaos spam again.
